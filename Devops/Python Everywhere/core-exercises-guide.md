# Core Exercises Form & Safety Guide

## 1. Reverse Crunches
### Proper Form
- Lie on your back, legs extended
- Bend knees and lift hips off the ground
- Curl lower back and hips towards chest
- Keep lower back pressed into the floor

### Common Mistakes to Avoid
- Using momentum instead of muscle control
- Lifting shoulders off the ground
- Arching lower back

### Injury Prevention
- Move slowly and controlled
- Keep core engaged
- Stop if you feel lower back strain
- Start with fewer repetitions

## 2. Plank Holds
### Proper Form
- Forearms on ground, elbows under shoulders
- Body forms straight line from head to heels
- Engage core, glutes, and quads
- Look slightly ahead of hands

### Common Mistakes to Avoid
- Sagging hips
- Lifting buttocks too high
- Dropping head
- Holding breath

### Injury Prevention
- Build up hold time gradually
- Maintain proper alignment
- Stop if you feel lower back pain
- Use modification (knees down) if needed

## 3. Russian Twists
### Proper Form
- Sit with knees bent, feet off ground
- Lean back slightly, creating V-shape
- Rotate torso side to side
- Optional: Hold weight or medicine ball

### Common Mistakes to Avoid
- Rounding back
- Using momentum instead of control
- Not fully rotating
- Holding breath

### Injury Prevention
- Keep spine neutral
- Move slowly and deliberately
- Start without weight
- Stop if you feel lower back strain

## 4. Side Planks
### Proper Form
- Lie on side, prop up on elbow
- Stack feet or stagger for stability
- Lift hips, creating straight line
- Engage core and side muscles

### Common Mistakes to Avoid
- Rotating torso
- Dropping hips
- Placing elbow too far from body
- Straining neck

### Injury Prevention
- Start with shorter holds
- Use knee modification if needed
- Maintain straight line
- Switch sides equally

## 5. Bird Dogs
### Proper Form
- Start on hands and knees
- Extend opposite arm and leg
- Keep back flat
- Move slowly and controlled

### Common Mistakes to Avoid
- Rotating hips
- Arching lower back
- Moving too quickly
- Losing balance

### Injury Prevention
- Move with control
- Keep core engaged
- Start with shorter extensions
- Use wall for initial balance

## 6. Dead Bugs
### Proper Form
- Lie on back, arms extended up
- Legs in tabletop position
- Lower opposite arm and leg
- Keep lower back pressed down

### Common Mistakes to Avoid
- Lifting lower back
- Losing core engagement
- Moving too quickly
- Uneven arm/leg movement

### Injury Prevention
- Move slowly
- Focus on core control
- Start with smaller movements
- Stop if you feel lower back strain

## General Core Training Safety Tips
- Warm up before exercising
- Progress gradually
- Listen to your body
- Maintain proper breathing
- Rest between workouts
- Consult professional if pain persists
