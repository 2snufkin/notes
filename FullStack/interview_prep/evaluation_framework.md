# Evaluation Framework - Technical Interview Assessment

## Skill Level Assessment Criteria

### **Junior Level (0-2 years)**

- Basic understanding of core concepts
- Can write simple, working code
- Needs guidance on best practices
- Limited experience with complex systems

### **Mid Level (2-4 years)**

- Solid understanding of core concepts
- Can design and implement features independently
- Understands trade-offs and best practices
- Experience with production systems
- Can debug and optimize code

### **Senior Level (4+ years)**

- Deep understanding of systems and architecture
- Can design complex, scalable solutions
- Strong problem-solving and debugging skills
- Experience with distributed systems
- Can mentor and guide others
- Understands business impact of technical decisions

## **Overall Assessment Matrix**

### **Scoring System**

- **1-2 points**: Junior level understanding
- **3-4 points**: Mid level understanding  
- **5 points**: Senior level understanding

### **Skill Level Determination**

**Junior Level (0-2 years)**

- Total score: 15-30 points
- Most answers at junior level
- Limited senior-level responses

**Mid Level (2-4 years)**

- Total score: 31-45 points
- Mix of junior and mid-level responses
- Some senior-level understanding

**Senior Level (4+ years)**

- Total score: 46-60 points
- Strong mid-level responses
- Multiple senior-level responses
- Demonstrates deep understanding

## **Preparation Plan Customization**

Based on the assessment, the preparation plan will focus on:

1. **Strengths**: Areas where you scored well
2. **Gaps**: Areas needing improvement
3. **Interview Focus**: Questions tailored to your level
4. **Practical Exercises**: Hands-on coding challenges
5. **System Design**: Architecture discussions
6. **Behavioral**: Leadership and collaboration scenarios
